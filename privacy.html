Privacy Policy

[App Name] · Effective October 18, 2026

[App Name] ("the App," "we," "us") is a desktop tool used by a single gaming content creator to turn highlight moments from gaming livestream recordings into short-form clips, review them in a private approval dashboard, and post approved clips to the creator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives:

  • Basic profile information (your TikTok display name, avatar, and account identifier), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to post the clips you approve.

When you post a clip, the App sends the video file and the title, caption, and posting settings you choose to TikTok. The App also stores the video clips it creates and your approve or reject decisions on your own computer to improve which clips it suggests.

The App does not collect information about TikTok users other than the connected account, and it does not access your followers, messages, or viewing history.

2. How we use information

  • To connect your account and show which account clips will be posted to.
  • To upload and publish the clips you approve, with the settings you choose.
  • To show the upload status of each clip in the dashboard.

Nothing is posted without your approval.

3. Where information is stored

The App runs locally on your computer. Tokens, profile information, clips, and logs are stored on that computer only. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information. Information is sent only to the platforms you choose to post to (TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram) through their official APIs, and their use of it is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Information stays on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect your TikTok account in the App, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings under security and permissions. Deleting the App's folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Security

Tokens and credentials are stored outside the App's code and are never published or shared. No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect this information.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
